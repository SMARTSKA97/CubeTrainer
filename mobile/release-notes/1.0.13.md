## What's new in 1.0.13

- **Coach me** (More menu, and a step in Today's session): a real scramble that you solve your way on the on-screen cube. The coach sees which stage you are in (cross, F2L, yellow cross, yellow face, corners, edges), explains what that stage is for, and gives a hint or the exact moves only when you ask. For an F2L pair it finds the easiest pair and tells you where the corner and edge are; for the last layer it recognises the case and names it. At the end you see how many moves each stage took.
- **Today's session** on Today and Learn: three steps (memory check, new material, a coached solve) with a day streak. One tap on the highlighted step.
- The practice cube has the wide and middle-layer moves (r, f, M) that the last-layer algorithms need.
