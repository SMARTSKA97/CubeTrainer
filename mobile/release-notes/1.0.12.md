## What's new in 1.0.12

- **Update check is now easy to find.** Preferences has an "App updates" card (works without an account), a dot appears on More and Preferences when a new version is out, and the app re-checks every 2 minutes instead of 10.
- **Lessons are much shorter.** Each lesson page is one screen: a short explanation, an optional "Watch it", and one practice on the cube. No separate read/try pages.
- **One-tap moves.** The practice cube has all 18 moves on screen (R, R', R2 …) instead of choosing a direction first.
- **Algorithms start straight away.** Opening a case step goes directly to the first algorithm; "Watch it" and "Try it" are buttons, the tips are gone.
