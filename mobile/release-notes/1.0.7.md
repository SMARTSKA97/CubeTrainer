- The cube animation now opens as a sheet on phones and tablets, so the timer is always within reach. On laptops it sits in the page.
- Laptops and desktops use the full width: the timer stays in view beside the scramble and the case.
- Updates no longer has its own entry in More; use Settings.
- No more background syncing every minute, so the app stops waking the server when idle.
- Today: tap any case in your plan to open it directly in the trainer. Redesigned with a daily-goal ring, streak flame with a seven-day strip, and XP levels.
- Daily challenge: one full scramble per day (the same for everyone), solved with the timer. Earns bonus XP.
- Streaks and XP: points for every solve, a bonus for the daily scramble and for each day of your streak, and levels with titles.
- OLL+PLL cases are now shown and played as two steps (orient, then permute) instead of one long jumble.
- Algorithm player has a running clock, so you can play a solution and follow it on your own cube against the time.
